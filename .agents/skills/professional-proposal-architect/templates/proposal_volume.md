## Section Structure

### 1. Opening Statement (Benefit-Led)

> Open with the customer's need and the outcome your approach delivers.

```
❌ "[Company] has extensive experience in..."
✅ "Your [project objective] will be achieved through [approach], delivering [measurable outcome]."
```

### 2. Understanding of Requirements

- Demonstrate deep comprehension of the RFP requirement
- Reflect customer's language and pain points
- Reference Value Proposition Canvas: Customer Jobs → Pains → Gains

### 3. Proposed Methodology / Approach

- Step-by-step approach with clear deliverables per phase
- Feature → Advantage → Benefit chain for every capability claim
- **Ghosting Integration Point:** Highlight proprietary approach advantages vs. standard industry methods

### 4. Risk Mitigation

- Identify top 3–5 risks specific to this scope
- Map each risk to a concrete mitigation action
- **Proof Insertion Point:** Cite past performance where you successfully mitigated similar risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Proof |
|:-----|:-----------|:-------|:-----------|:------|
| [Risk 1] | H/M/L | H/M/L | [Specific action] | [Past performance / case study] |

### 5. Staffing & Resources (if applicable)

- Key personnel with relevant qualifications
- **Ghosting Integration Point:** Emphasize retention strategy, proprietary bench depth
- Org chart with clear reporting lines

### 6. Schedule / Timeline

- Gantt chart or milestone table
- Critical path highlighted
- Dependencies and handoff points

### 7. Value-Add / Innovation

- Exceed baseline RFP requirements (Gain Creators from Value Map)
- Quantified additional value
- **Ghosting Integration Point:** Features that competitors cannot match

### 8. Graphics

- Minimum ONE strategic graphic per major section
- **Action Caption Rule:** Every graphic MUST have a caption telling the reader what conclusion to draw
