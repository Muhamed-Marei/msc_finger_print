# Executive Summary — Four-Box Template

> **Format:** Industry-standard Four-Box structure (Shipley/APMP).
> **Timing:** Write early in capture phase. Present at kickoff meeting as internal compass.
> **Audience:** Senior executive decision-makers — often the ONLY section they read.

## Box 1: Customer Needs

> *Establishes empathy — proves you understand the client's operational reality.*

**Instructions:**
- Reflect the customer's own language (from RFP, meetings, public statements)
- Acknowledge ownership of the problem — show purposeful research
- Cover functional, social, and emotional jobs (from Value Proposition Canvas)

| [Need 2] | [Source] | [Rating] |
| [Need 3] | [Source] | [Rating] |

**Narrative:** [2-3 paragraphs opening with the customer's operational context, NOT your company history]

## Box 2: Outcomes and Vision

> *Articulates the desired future state — connects proposal to customer's strategic vision.*

**Instructions:**
- Focus on measurable outcomes (revenue, uptime, compliance, timeline)
- Use the customer's KPIs and success metrics
- Paint the TO-BE state without mechanism details

| Desired Outcome | Metric | Target |
|:----------------|:-------|:-------|
| [Outcome 1] | [KPI] | [Quantified target] |
| [Outcome 2] | [KPI] | [Target] |
| [Outcome 3] | [KPI] | [Target] |

**Narrative:** [2-3 paragraphs describing the measurable future state the customer achieves]

---

## Box 3: The Solution

> *Concise, high-level overview — jargon-free for executive readers.*

**Instructions:**
- Eliminate confusing technical jargon
- Deep engineering details belong in technical volumes, NOT here
- Show the solution as the BRIDGE between Box 1 (current pain) and Box 2 (desired future)

**Narrative:** [2-3 paragraphs — methodology overview, approach philosophy, key differentiators at a high level]

**Solution Visual:** [Insert architecture diagram or process flow with ACTION CAPTION]

*Action Caption: "Figure X demonstrates how [methodology] directly addresses [customer need], delivering [measurable outcome] within [timeline]."*

---

## Box 4: Discriminators and Proof

> *Core competitive advantage + empirical evidence justifying selection.*

**Instructions:**
- Highlight unique value propositions (informed by ghosting strategy)
- Include ROI calculations
- Deploy proof from the 7 sources (research, metrics, cases, endorsements, testimonials, publicity, logic)
- Explain WHY you offer greater value than alternatives (without naming competitors)

| [Unique capability 3] | [Proof type] | [Evidence] |

**ROI Summary:** [1 paragraph with key financial return calculations]

## Quality Checklist

```
☐ Customer name appears 3× more than bidder name
☐ Every paragraph opens with buyer benefit, not bidder capability
☐ Active voice throughout — "will" not "can"
☐ Zero fluff words without proof backing
☐ All claims supported by ≥1 of 7 proof sources
☐ Graphics have action captions
☐ Jargon eliminated — readable by non-technical executive
☐ Win themes from capture plan are consistently threaded
☐ Ghosting strategy subtly integrated (Box 4 discriminators)
```
