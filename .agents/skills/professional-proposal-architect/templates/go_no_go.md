## Scoring Thresholds

| Total Weighted Score | Recommendation | Action |
|:---------------------|:---------------|:-------|
| **≥ 90** | **STRONG GO** | Full capture commitment, assign resources |
| **70–89** | **CONDITIONAL GO** | Proceed with identified mitigation actions |
| **50–69** | **CONDITIONAL NO-GO** | Pursue only if specific conditions change |
| **< 50** | **NO-GO** | Do not pursue — redirect resources |

---

## Risk Flags (Automatic No-Go Triggers)

```
🚩 Missing mandatory certification with no path to obtain before deadline
🚩 No customer relationship and no teaming partner with access
🚩 Compliance documentation gaps that cannot be resolved (e.g., legal entity not established)
🚩 Contract terms requiring unlimited liability or uninsurable risk
🚩 Bid bond requirements exceeding available financial capacity
🚩 Anti-collusion conflict (shared ownership with another bidder)
```
